Brush Hogging

Heavy-Duty Mowing For Pastures And Fields

Brush hogging is heavy-duty vegetation control for pastures, fields, and overgrown land where there are few saplings. It cuts down tall grass, weeds, and light brush that a regular mower can't handle.

Keep Open Ground Open

Stop Pastures From Turning Into Forests

Left alone, a pasture fills in fast. Grass and weeds come first, then brush, then saplings. In a few years, open ground starts turning back into woods.

We recommend brush hogging 1 to 2 times per year. Regular cuts keep your pasture or field open, easy to walk, and ready to use.

If saplings and thick brush have already moved in, forestry mulching or field reclamation may be the better first step. We'll tell you which one your land needs when we quote it.
