Routes For Walking, Hunting, And Maintenance

Trail work starts with where you need to go and how you plan to use the route. A walking path, UTV trail, hunting lane, and equipment access route all call for a slightly different approach.

We consider grade, wet areas, turning space, visibility, and how the trail connects to the rest of the property before clearing.

The finished result should make your land easier to move through without clearing more than needed or leaving a route that is difficult to maintain.
