Clear Openings, Edges, And Access Routes

Food plot areas often need more than a small clearing. Access lanes, shaded edges, invasive growth, and encroaching saplings can all affect how usable the area is.

We can help open the site, clean up approach routes, push back brush, and make the area easier to work with before soil prep or planting begins.

Every property is different, so we focus on creating a practical opening that fits the terrain, surrounding cover, and how you plan to use the land.
